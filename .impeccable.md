# Impeccable — Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Dutch homeowners (plus some small-business owners) in Oudewater, Woerden, Utrecht and the
surrounding region who are considering a high-value, carefully-researched home upgrade:
heat pumps (warmtepompen), solar (zonnepanelen), home batteries (thuisbatterijen), air
conditioning (airco), fuse-box / electrical work (meterkast & elektra), and broader home
sustainability (verduurzaming). They arrive mid-research, usually comparing several
installers, weighing a €5k–€15k+ decision. Many browse on mobile, often in the evening, and
are looking for reassurance before they make contact. Primary language is **Dutch**, with
**English** as a secondary locale.

### Brand Personality
MMC Techniek B.V. — an established (16+ years), certified (NEN-1010, NEN-3140, VCA), local
installer near Utrecht. Voice: precise, technical, dependable, no-nonsense, quietly premium.
Three words: **trustworthy, precise, local**. The single emotional job of the interface: make
a homeowner feel this is a *safe, competent, certified expert who will not mess up their home*.
Trust & competence over flash.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Precision studio" — an editorial, technical-industrial look built on Barlow Condensed
uppercase display type, a bright engineering blue (`#42a8f2`, deep `#1a7bc1`), slate
neutrals, hairline rules, tabular numbers, and restrained "aurora" accents. **Keep this DNA.**
Direction for this work: **refine, not redesign** — perfect the execution (true OKLCH color,
brand-tinted neutrals, WCAG-AA contrast, a real spacing + type scale, consistent components),
strip out AI-slop tells, and fix functional/technical defects. Light theme only (correct for
daytime homeowner research on desktop and mobile).

Anti-references (what it must NOT look like): generic SaaS/AI installer templates, cyan-on-black
neon, purple→blue hero gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, endless identical icon-card grids.

### Design Principles
1. **Trust is the product.** Every decision should raise perceived competence and safety —
   legible contrast, real proof (certs, projects, reviews), zero broken details.
2. **Refine, don't reinvent.** Preserve the blue + condensed-uppercase identity; elevate its
   execution to production grade.
3. **One system, no drift.** A single source of truth for color, spacing, type, icons and copy
   (design tokens + i18n) — eliminate the duplicated palettes, icon sets and hardcoded strings.
4. **Guide to the quote.** The quote request is the primary action; hierarchy, CTAs and empty
   states all funnel there, with call and WhatsApp as clear secondary paths.
5. **Accessible & fast by default.** WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, self-hosted
   fonts, server-first rendering, dvh-safe mobile layouts.
